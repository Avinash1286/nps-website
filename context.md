### Content

Nepal Police School, Garuda, Rautahat (Nepali: नेपाल पुलिस स्कुल, गरुडा, रौतहट) is a Nepal Police school located in Garuda Municipality–8, Basbitti Jingadiya, Rautahat District, Madhesh Province, Nepal. Government education records identify the school as Nepal Police School Garuda, Rautahat, classify it as a private school, and give the IEMIS/school code 320120002.

The school is run within the Nepal Police welfare system. Nepal Police describes its school network as having been created to educate children of police personnel and as also serving children from the general public. The Garuda school must not be confused with Nepal Police School Sanga, Samakhushi, Dharan, Chitwan, Tanahun, Dang, Surkhet, Dhangadhi, or another Nepal Police School branch. Do not use another branch's address, leaders, telephone numbers, admission rules, or notices.

Institutional historical sources report that the Garuda school was established on 25 March 2002 AD, corresponding to 2058 BS. The school's 2026 annual publication described its 24th anniversary, corroborating a 2002 founding year. The local school publication uses the motto “Better Schooling for Ideal Citizenship.” If the school later publishes a different official English wording, use the newest admin-approved wording.

The school's stated educational purpose centres on quality education, discipline, character, responsibility, moral values, creativity, and the development of capable citizens. Avoid promotional superlatives or rankings unless a current, verifiable source is published.

## Research decisions and excluded claims

The following details were deliberately not made durable AI facts:

- Fixed enrollment totals, because available IEMIS/CEHRD totals differ by academic year and become stale.
- Current Grade 11–12 streams, because Grade 11–12 operation is supported but current stream names were not reliably confirmed.
- Current leadership names, because roles change and leadership already belongs in the website's admin-managed Leadership section.
- Current fees, admission dates, document lists, hostel charges, transport routes, office hours, results, book lists, and vacancies.
- Marketing claims about Wi-Fi, CCTV, transport, “world-class” facilities, or specific laboratories that lacked sufficiently strong current confirmation.
- Legacy school email addresses whose domain spelling and current operation could not be verified.
- Historical land area, donor names, and “20 km south of Chandranigahapur” directions, because these appeared in an older institutional profile and should be confirmed locally before publication.
